Wagonwheel Lake Trail

Wagonwheel Lake Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.1 mi out and back) near Hoodsport, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,140 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.1 mi round trip
  • About 3,140 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
