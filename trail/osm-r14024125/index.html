Unicorn Peak

Unicorn Peak is a 2.3 mi trail (4.5 mi out and back) near Packwood, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,390 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,390 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
