Green Mountain Trail #110

Green Mountain Trail #110 is a 5.5 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Packwood, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,200 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 2,200 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
