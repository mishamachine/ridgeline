Sauk Lake Trail

Sauk Lake Trail is a 1.4 mi trail (2.8 mi out and back) near Concrete, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,230 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 2.8 mi round trip
  • About 1,230 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
