King's Peak Trail

King's Peak Trail is a 3.1 mi trail (6.3 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,320 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 6.3 mi round trip
  • About 3,320 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
