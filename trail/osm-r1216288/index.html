Dry Creek Falls Hike

Dry Creek Falls Hike is a 4.2 mi trail (8.4 mi out and back) near Stevenson, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,080 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.4 mi round trip
  • About 1,080 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
