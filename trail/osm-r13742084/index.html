Mount Teneriffe Trail

Mount Teneriffe Trail is a 6.4 mi trail (13 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,750 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 13 mi round trip
  • About 3,750 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
