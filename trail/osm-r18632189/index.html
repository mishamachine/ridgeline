Columbia Mountain Loop

Columbia Mountain Loop is a 2.0 mi trail (3.9 mi out and back) near Republic, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 610 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.
