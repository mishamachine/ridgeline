Dog Mountain Trails

Dog Mountain Trails is a 6.9 mi trail (14 mi out and back) near Stevenson, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,690 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 14 mi round trip
  • About 3,690 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
