Franklin Falls Trail

Franklin Falls Trail is a 1.0 mi trail (2.0 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 340 ft of gain, computed level easy. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.0 mi round trip
  • About 340 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
