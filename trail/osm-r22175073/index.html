Timber Ridge Trail

Timber Ridge Trail is a 2.9 mi trail (5.8 mi out and back) near Republic, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,640 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 5.8 mi round trip
  • About 1,640 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
