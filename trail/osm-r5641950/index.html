Chain Lakes Trail

Chain Lakes Trail is a 3.9 mi trail (7.8 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,320 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 7.8 mi round trip
  • About 2,320 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
