Huckleberry Mountain Trail

Huckleberry Mountain Trail is a 6.8 mi trail (14 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 4,980 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 14 mi round trip
  • About 4,980 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
