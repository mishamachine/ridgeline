Rainy Lake Trail

Rainy Lake Trail is a 3.0 mi trail (6.0 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,730 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 6.0 mi round trip
  • About 2,730 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
