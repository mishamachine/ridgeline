Deception Pass Trail

Deception Pass Trail is a 4.7 mi trail (9.4 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,150 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.4 mi round trip
  • About 1,150 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
