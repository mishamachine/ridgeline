Lake Constance Route

Lake Constance Route is a 1.6 mi trail (3.3 mi out and back) near Brinnon, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,130 ft of gain, computed level moderate.

  • Out and back: 3.3 mi round trip
  • About 3,130 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
