Bunker Ridge Trail

Bunker Ridge Trail is a 2.8 mi trail (5.5 mi out and back) near Port Angeles, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 830 ft of gain, computed level moderate.

  • Out and back: 5.5 mi round trip
  • About 830 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
