Talapus Lake Trail

Talapus Lake Trail is a 3.2 mi trail (6.3 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,250 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.3 mi round trip
  • About 1,250 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
