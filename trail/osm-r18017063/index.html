Panorama Point Trail

Panorama Point Trail is a 1.9 mi trail (3.8 mi out and back) near Packwood, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,440 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.8 mi round trip
  • About 1,440 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
