Monkey Bear Falls Trail

Monkey Bear Falls Trail is a 2.1 mi trail (4.3 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 580 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 4.3 mi round trip
  • About 580 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
