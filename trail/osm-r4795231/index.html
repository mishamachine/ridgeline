Mount Defiance Trail

Mount Defiance Trail is a 5.9 mi trail (12 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,840 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 12 mi round trip
  • About 1,840 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed
