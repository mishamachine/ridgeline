Mount Townsend Trail #839

Mount Townsend Trail #839 is a 6.1 mi trail (12 mi out and back) near Quilcene, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,350 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 12 mi round trip
  • About 3,350 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
