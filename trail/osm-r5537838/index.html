Mount Forgotten Trail

Mount Forgotten Trail is a 5.8 mi trail (12 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,950 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 12 mi round trip
  • About 3,950 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
