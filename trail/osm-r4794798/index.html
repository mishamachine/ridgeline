Dorothy Lake Trail

Dorothy Lake Trail is a 4.3 mi trail (8.6 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,600 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.6 mi round trip
  • About 1,600 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed
