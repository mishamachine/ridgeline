Esmeralda Basin Trail

Esmeralda Basin Trail is a 4.6 mi trail (9.1 mi out and back) near Cle Elum, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,640 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,640 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
