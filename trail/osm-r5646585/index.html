French Ridge Trail

French Ridge Trail is a 7.3 mi trail (15 mi out and back) near Leavenworth, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,760 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 15 mi round trip
  • About 2,760 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed
