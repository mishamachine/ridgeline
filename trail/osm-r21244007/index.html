Blazed Ridge Trail

Blazed Ridge Trail is a 3.1 mi trail (6.2 mi out and back) near Cle Elum, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,520 ft of gain, computed level moderate.

  • Out and back: 6.2 mi round trip
  • About 1,520 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
