Marie Lakes Trail

Marie Lakes Trail is a 1.6 mi trail (3.2 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 880 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 3.2 mi round trip
  • About 880 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
