Peek-A-Boo Lake Trail

Peek-A-Boo Lake Trail is a 2.0 mi trail (4.1 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,170 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,170 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
