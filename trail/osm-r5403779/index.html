Snoqualmie Falls Trail

Snoqualmie Falls Trail is a 0.7 mi trail (1.3 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 380 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: a waterfall. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 1.3 mi round trip
  • About 380 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a waterfall
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
