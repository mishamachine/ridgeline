Cady Ridge Trail

Cady Ridge Trail is a 6.4 mi trail (13 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,600 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 13 mi round trip
  • About 2,600 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline
