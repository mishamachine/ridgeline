Spade Lake Trail

Spade Lake Trail is a 3.7 mi trail (7.4 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,500 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 7.4 mi round trip
  • About 2,500 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
