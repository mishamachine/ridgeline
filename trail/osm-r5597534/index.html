Found Lake Trail

Found Lake Trail is a 6.4 mi trail (13 mi out and back) near Marblemount, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,600 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall.

  • Out and back: 13 mi round trip
  • About 3,600 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall
