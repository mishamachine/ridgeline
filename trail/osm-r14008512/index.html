Long Meadow Trail

Long Meadow Trail is a 2.2 mi trail (4.5 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 590 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed.

  • Out and back: 4.5 mi round trip
  • About 590 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed
