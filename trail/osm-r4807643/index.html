Blanca Lake Trail

Blanca Lake Trail is a 3.8 mi trail (7.5 mi out and back) near Index, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,650 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,650 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
