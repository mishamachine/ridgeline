Brohm Lake Trail

Brohm Lake Trail is a 2.2 mi loop in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 400 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Loop: 2.2 mi around
  • About 400 ft gain, level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
