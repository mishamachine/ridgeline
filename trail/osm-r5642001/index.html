Square Lake Trail

Square Lake Trail is a 4.8 mi trail (9.6 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,920 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 9.6 mi round trip
  • About 1,920 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed
