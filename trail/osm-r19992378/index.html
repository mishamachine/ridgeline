Fuller Mountain Trail

Fuller Mountain Trail is a 1.7 mi trail (3.4 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 910 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 3.4 mi round trip
  • About 910 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
