Hamilton Mountain Trail

Hamilton Mountain Trail is a 3.6 mi trail (7.1 mi out and back) near Stevenson, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,040 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.1 mi round trip
  • About 2,040 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
