Colchuck Lake Trail

Colchuck Lake Trail is a 4.6 mi trail (9.1 mi out and back) near Leavenworth, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,180 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: no dogs. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.1 mi round trip
  • About 2,180 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: no dogs
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
