Island Lake Trail

Island Lake Trail is a 5.6 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,680 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 2,680 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline

Directions are a guide; check conditions; not for emergency use.
