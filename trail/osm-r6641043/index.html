Carne Mountain Trail

Carne Mountain Trail is a 3.7 mi trail (7.4 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,520 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.4 mi round trip
  • About 3,520 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline

Directions are a guide; check conditions; not for emergency use.
