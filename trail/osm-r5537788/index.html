Elliot Creek (Goat Lake) Trail

Elliot Creek (Goat Lake) Trail is a 5.7 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 880 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 880 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
