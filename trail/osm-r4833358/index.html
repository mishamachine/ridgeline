Bell Pass Trail

Bell Pass Trail is a 5.2 mi trail (10 mi out and back) near Concrete, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,900 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 1,900 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed
