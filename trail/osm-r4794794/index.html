High Lakes Trail

High Lakes Trail is a 3.8 mi trail (7.5 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,180 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 7.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,180 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed
