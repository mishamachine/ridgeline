Iron Peak Trail

Iron Peak Trail is a 3.3 mi trail (6.6 mi out and back) near Cle Elum, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,200 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.6 mi round trip
  • About 2,200 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline

Directions are a guide; check conditions; not for emergency use.
