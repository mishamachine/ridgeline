Hidden Lake Trail

Hidden Lake Trail is a 3.9 mi trail (7.8 mi out and back) near Marblemount, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,250 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 7.8 mi round trip
  • About 3,250 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
