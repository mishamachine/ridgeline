Vermilion Peak Route

Vermilion Peak Route is a 2.4 mi trail (4.7 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,810 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 4.7 mi round trip
  • About 3,810 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
