Circle Peak Trail

Circle Peak Trail is a 3.8 mi trail (7.7 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,920 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 7.7 mi round trip
  • About 1,920 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
