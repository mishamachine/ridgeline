Mount Stakes Trail

Mount Stakes Trail is a 5.2 mi trail (10 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,170 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 3,170 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
