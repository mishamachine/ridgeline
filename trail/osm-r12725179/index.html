Sea to Summit

Sea to Summit is a 3.5 mi trail (6.9 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,810 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,810 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
