Summit Meadow Trail

Summit Meadow Trail is a 2.7 mi trail (5.4 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,410 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 5.4 mi round trip
  • About 1,410 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
