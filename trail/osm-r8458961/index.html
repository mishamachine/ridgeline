Illahee Preserve Trails

Illahee Preserve Trails is a 2.3 mi trail (4.5 mi out and back) near Bremerton, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 350 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.5 mi round trip
  • About 350 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
