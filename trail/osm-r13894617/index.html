Lost Lake Trail

Lost Lake Trail is a 2.8 mi trail (5.5 mi out and back) near Sultan, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 630 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.5 mi round trip
  • About 630 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
