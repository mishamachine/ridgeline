Falls Creek Trail

Falls Creek Trail is a 3.5 mi trail (6.9 mi out and back) near Leavenworth, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,600 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 6.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,600 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed
