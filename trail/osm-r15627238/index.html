Muddy Meadows Trail #13

Muddy Meadows Trail #13 is a 4.8 mi trail (9.5 mi out and back) near Packwood, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,450 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,450 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
