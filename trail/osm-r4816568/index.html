Jack Mountain Trail

Jack Mountain Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.2 mi out and back) near Newhalem, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,840 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 8.2 mi round trip
  • About 3,840 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
