North Rim #1

North Rim #1 is a 2.7 mi trail (5.5 mi out and back) near Olympia, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 810 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.5 mi round trip
  • About 810 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
