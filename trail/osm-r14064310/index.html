Ridge Trail

Ridge Trail is a 3.4 mi trail (6.8 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,250 ft of gain, computed level moderate.

  • Out and back: 6.8 mi round trip
  • About 1,250 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
