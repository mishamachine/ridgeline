Tomyhoi Lake Trail

Tomyhoi Lake Trail is a 3.8 mi trail (7.6 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,680 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 7.6 mi round trip
  • About 1,680 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
