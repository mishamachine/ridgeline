Winchester Mountain Trail

Winchester Mountain Trail is a 1.7 mi trail (3.5 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,240 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,240 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
