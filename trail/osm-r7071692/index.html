Blowdown Mountain Trail

Blowdown Mountain Trail is a 1.2 mi trail (2.3 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 640 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 2.3 mi round trip
  • About 640 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
