Chelan Butte Trail

Chelan Butte Trail is a 3.7 mi trail (7.4 mi out and back) near Chelan, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,610 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.4 mi round trip
  • About 2,610 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
