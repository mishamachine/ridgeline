Ashland Lakes Trail

Ashland Lakes Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.2 mi out and back) near Granite Falls, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 500 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.2 mi round trip
  • About 500 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline
