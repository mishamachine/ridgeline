Sheep Lake Trail

Sheep Lake Trail is a 1.9 mi trail (3.7 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 580 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 3.7 mi round trip
  • About 580 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
