Damfino Lakes Trail

Damfino Lakes Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.1 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,230 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,230 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
