Hester Lake Trail

Hester Lake Trail is a 2.3 mi trail (4.5 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,090 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 4.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,090 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: dogs allowed
