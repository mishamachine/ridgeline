Lorraine Ridge Trail

Lorraine Ridge Trail is a 3.4 mi trail (6.8 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,260 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 6.8 mi round trip
  • About 2,260 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed
