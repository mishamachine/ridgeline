Grouse Basin Loop Path

Grouse Basin Loop Path is a 1.0 mi trail (2.0 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 160 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: views, a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.0 mi round trip
  • About 160 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
