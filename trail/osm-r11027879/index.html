Kaleetan Peak Trail

Kaleetan Peak Trail is a 5.4 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,840 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 3,840 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
