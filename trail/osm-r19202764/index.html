Mimi Falls Path

Mimi Falls Path is a 1.8 mi trail (3.7 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 190 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed.

  • Out and back: 3.7 mi round trip
  • About 190 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed
