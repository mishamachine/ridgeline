Mokelumne Coast to Crest Trail

Mokelumne Coast to Crest Trail is a 3.7 mi trail (7.4 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 730 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.4 mi round trip
  • About 730 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
