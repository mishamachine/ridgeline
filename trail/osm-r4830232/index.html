Table Mountain Trail

Table Mountain Trail is a 1.4 mi trail (2.8 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 730 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.8 mi round trip
  • About 730 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
