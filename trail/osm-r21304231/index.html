Lookout Mountain Trail

Lookout Mountain Trail is a 1.5 mi trail (3.1 mi out and back) near Twisp, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,300 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 3.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,300 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
