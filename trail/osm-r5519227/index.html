Poo Poo Point Trail

Poo Poo Point Trail is a 3.3 mi trail (6.6 mi out and back) near Issaquah, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,730 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.6 mi round trip
  • About 1,730 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
