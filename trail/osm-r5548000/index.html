Mount Pugh Trail

Mount Pugh Trail is a 4.2 mi trail (8.5 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,660 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.5 mi round trip
  • About 3,660 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
