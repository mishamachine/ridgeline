Eagle Pass Trail

Eagle Pass Trail is a 1.1 mi trail (2.2 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 730 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 2.2 mi round trip
  • About 730 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
