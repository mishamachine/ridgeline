Thomas Lake Trail #111

Thomas Lake Trail #111 is a 3.2 mi trail (6.5 mi out and back) near Trout Lake, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 790 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.5 mi round trip
  • About 790 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
