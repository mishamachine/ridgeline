McClellan Meadows Trail #157

McClellan Meadows Trail #157 is a 3.0 mi trail (6.0 mi out and back) near Trout Lake, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 580 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.
