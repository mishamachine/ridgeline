Ancient Lakes Trail

Ancient Lakes Trail is a 2.1 mi trail (4.1 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 170 ft of gain, computed level easy. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.1 mi round trip
  • About 170 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline

Directions are a guide; check conditions; not for emergency use.
