Lake Christine Trail #249

Lake Christine Trail #249 is a 1.7 mi trail (3.5 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 750 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.
