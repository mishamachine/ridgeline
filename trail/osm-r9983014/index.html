Needle Peak

Needle Peak is a 2.7 mi trail (5.4 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,690 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.4 mi round trip
  • About 2,690 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
