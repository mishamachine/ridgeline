Norse Peak Trail

Norse Peak Trail is a 4.3 mi trail (8.5 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,560 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,560 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
