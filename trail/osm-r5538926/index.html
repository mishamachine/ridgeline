Red Mountain Trail

Red Mountain Trail is a 0.8 mi trail (1.6 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 650 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 1.6 mi round trip
  • About 650 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
