McDonald Mountain Trail

McDonald Mountain Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.2 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,340 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.2 mi round trip
  • About 2,340 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
