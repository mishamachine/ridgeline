Fragrance Lake Trail

Fragrance Lake Trail is a 2.3 mi trail (4.7 mi out and back) near Bellingham, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,200 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.7 mi round trip
  • About 1,200 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
