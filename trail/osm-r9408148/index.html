Bosumarne Falls Trail

Bosumarne Falls Trail is a 0.7 mi trail (1.5 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 560 ft of gain, computed level easy. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 1.5 mi round trip
  • About 560 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
