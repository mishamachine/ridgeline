Gable Lakes Trail

Gable Lakes Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.1 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,320 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.1 mi round trip
  • About 3,320 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
