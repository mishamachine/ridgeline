Mt Myra Trail

Mt Myra Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.1 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,550 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 5.1 mi round trip
  • About 2,550 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
