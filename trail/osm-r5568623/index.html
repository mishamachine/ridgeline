Beaver Lake Trail

Beaver Lake Trail is a 1.3 mi trail (2.5 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 230 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.5 mi round trip
  • About 230 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
