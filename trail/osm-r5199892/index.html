Emerald Ridge Trail

Emerald Ridge Trail is a 5.7 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,710 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 1,710 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
