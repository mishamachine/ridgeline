Walupt Lake Trail #101

Walupt Lake Trail #101 is a 4.3 mi trail (8.5 mi out and back) near Packwood, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,070 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,070 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
