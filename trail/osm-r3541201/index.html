Sweeney Ridge Trail

Sweeney Ridge Trail is a 2.9 mi trail (5.8 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 680 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed.

  • Out and back: 5.8 mi round trip
  • About 680 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed
