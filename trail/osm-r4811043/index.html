Commonwealth Basin Trail

Commonwealth Basin Trail is a 2.0 mi trail (4.1 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,530 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 4.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,530 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: dogs allowed
