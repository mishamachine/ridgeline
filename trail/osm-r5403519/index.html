Gumjuwac Trail

Gumjuwac Trail is a 4.5 mi trail (8.9 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,690 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.9 mi round trip
  • About 1,690 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
