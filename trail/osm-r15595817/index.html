Nannie Ridge Trail #98

Nannie Ridge Trail #98 is a 4.5 mi trail (8.9 mi out and back) near Packwood, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,040 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,040 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
