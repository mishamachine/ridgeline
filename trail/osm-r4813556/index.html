Hannegan Peak Trail

Hannegan Peak Trail is a 5.2 mi trail (10 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,070 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 3,070 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline
