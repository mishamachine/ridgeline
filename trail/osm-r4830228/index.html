Chain Lakes Trail

Chain Lakes Trail is a 5.5 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,720 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 1,720 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
