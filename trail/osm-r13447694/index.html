Sauer's Mountain Trail

Sauer's Mountain Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.1 mi out and back) near Leavenworth, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,780 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,780 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
