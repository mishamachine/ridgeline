Greek Creek Basin Trail

Greek Creek Basin Trail is a 2.8 mi trail (5.6 mi out and back) near Cle Elum, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,070 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 5.6 mi round trip
  • About 1,070 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
