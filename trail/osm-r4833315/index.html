Blue Lake Trail

Blue Lake Trail is a 0.7 mi trail (1.4 mi out and back) near Concrete, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 220 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 1.4 mi round trip
  • About 220 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
