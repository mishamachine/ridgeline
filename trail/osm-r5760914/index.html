Larch Lakes Trail

Larch Lakes Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.3 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,020 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 5.3 mi round trip
  • About 2,020 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
