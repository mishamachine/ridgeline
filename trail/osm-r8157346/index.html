Mt. McLoughlin Trail

Mt. McLoughlin Trail is a 4.4 mi trail (8.7 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,740 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.7 mi round trip
  • About 3,740 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
