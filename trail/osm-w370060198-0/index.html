Old Elsay Lake Trail

Old Elsay Lake Trail is a 4.6 mi trail (9.3 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,410 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 9.3 mi round trip
  • About 1,410 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
