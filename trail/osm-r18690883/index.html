Thompson Ridge

Thompson Ridge is a 4.5 mi trail (9.1 mi out and back) near Colville, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,590 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 9.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,590 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
