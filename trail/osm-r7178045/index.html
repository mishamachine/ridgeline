Dirty Harry's Peak Trail

Dirty Harry's Peak Trail is a 4.0 mi trail (7.9 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,300 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.9 mi round trip
  • About 3,300 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline

Directions are a guide; check conditions; not for emergency use.
