Avalanche Crest Trail

Avalanche Crest Trail is a 2.4 mi trail (4.9 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,640 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,640 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
