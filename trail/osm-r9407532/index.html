Lindeman Lake Trail

Lindeman Lake Trail is a 1.1 mi trail (2.1 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 710 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 2.1 mi round trip
  • About 710 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: dogs allowed
