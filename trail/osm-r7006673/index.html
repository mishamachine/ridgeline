Monitor Ridge Climbing Route

Monitor Ridge Climbing Route is a 4.3 mi trail (8.6 mi out and back) near Cougar, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 4,530 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 8.6 mi round trip
  • About 4,530 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
