Chilnualna Falls Trail

Chilnualna Falls Trail is a 3.9 mi trail (7.9 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,240 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed, no dogs. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,240 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed, no dogs
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
