Eagle Peak Trail

Eagle Peak Trail is a 3.3 mi trail (6.5 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,830 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,830 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline
