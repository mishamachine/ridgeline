Slide Lake Trail

Slide Lake Trail is a 1.5 mi trail (3.0 mi out and back) near Marblemount, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 700 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.0 mi round trip
  • About 700 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
