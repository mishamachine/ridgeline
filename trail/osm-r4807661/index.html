Beckler Peak Trail

Beckler Peak Trail is a 3.7 mi trail (7.5 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,240 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,240 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
