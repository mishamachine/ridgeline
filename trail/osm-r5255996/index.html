Goat Peak Trail

Goat Peak Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.3 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,710 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.3 mi round trip
  • About 2,710 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
