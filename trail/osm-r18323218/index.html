Sullivan Lake National Recreation Trail

Sullivan Lake National Recreation Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.3 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 530 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.3 mi round trip
  • About 530 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
