Kern Peak Trail

Kern Peak Trail is a 5.3 mi trail (11 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,730 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 2,730 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
