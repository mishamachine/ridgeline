Boston Ridge Trail

Boston Ridge Trail is a 4.5 mi trail (9.0 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,060 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 9.0 mi round trip
  • About 3,060 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
