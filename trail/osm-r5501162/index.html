Sheep Mountain Trail

Sheep Mountain Trail is a 3.7 mi trail (7.5 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 740 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 7.5 mi round trip
  • About 740 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
