Green Lake Trail

Green Lake Trail is a 4.7 mi trail (9.4 mi out and back) near Enumclaw, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,540 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.4 mi round trip
  • About 1,540 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
