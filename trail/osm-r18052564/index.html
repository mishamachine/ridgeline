Blue Lake Trail

Blue Lake Trail is a 2.3 mi trail (4.6 mi out and back) near Mazama, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 900 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.6 mi round trip
  • About 900 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
