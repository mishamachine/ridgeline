Mount Storm King Trail

Mount Storm King Trail is a 3.8 mi trail (7.6 mi out and back) near Port Angeles, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,820 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.6 mi round trip
  • About 3,820 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
