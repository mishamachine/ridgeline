Osborne Mountain Trail #250

Osborne Mountain Trail #250 is a 4.9 mi trail (9.7 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,840 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 9.7 mi round trip
  • About 2,840 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
