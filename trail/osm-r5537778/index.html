Pinnacle Lake Trail

Pinnacle Lake Trail is a 1.8 mi trail (3.7 mi out and back) near Granite Falls, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,210 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.7 mi round trip
  • About 1,210 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
