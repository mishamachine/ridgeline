Cottonwood Meadows Trail #705

Cottonwood Meadows Trail #705 is a 2.1 mi trail (4.2 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,140 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 4.2 mi round trip
  • About 1,140 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
