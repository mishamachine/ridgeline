Goat Mountain Trail

Goat Mountain Trail is a 4.6 mi trail (9.2 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 4,110 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.2 mi round trip
  • About 4,110 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
