Buck Creek Pass Trail

Buck Creek Pass Trail is a 4.7 mi trail (9.4 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,250 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 9.4 mi round trip
  • About 2,250 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
