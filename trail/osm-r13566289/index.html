Greenwood Lake Trail #253

Greenwood Lake Trail #253 is a 3.0 mi trail (6.0 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,180 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.0 mi round trip
  • About 1,180 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
