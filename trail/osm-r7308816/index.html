Baring Mountain Trail

Baring Mountain Trail is a 2.3 mi trail (4.5 mi out and back) near Index, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,290 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.5 mi round trip
  • About 3,290 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
