Crystal Lake Trail

Crystal Lake Trail is a 4.4 mi trail (8.9 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,290 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,290 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
