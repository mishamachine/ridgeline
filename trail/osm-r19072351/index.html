Sherwin Lakes Trail

Sherwin Lakes Trail is a 3.0 mi trail (5.9 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,050 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.9 mi round trip
  • About 1,050 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
