Goat Lake Trail

Goat Lake Trail is a 1.5 mi trail (2.9 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 560 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 2.9 mi round trip
  • About 560 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
