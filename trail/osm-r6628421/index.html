Lake Ethel Trail

Lake Ethel Trail is a 4.7 mi trail (9.3 mi out and back) near Leavenworth, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,290 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.3 mi round trip
