Dirty Harry's Balcony Trail

Dirty Harry's Balcony Trail is a 2.1 mi trail (4.2 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,350 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.2 mi round trip
  • About 1,350 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
