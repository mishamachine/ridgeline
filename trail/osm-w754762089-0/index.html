Thunder Mountain Lakes Trail

Thunder Mountain Lakes Trail is a 1.4 mi trail (2.8 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 810 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 2.8 mi round trip
  • About 810 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
