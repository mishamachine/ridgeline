Lost Lake Trail

Lost Lake Trail is a 4.9 mi trail (9.9 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,510 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 9.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,510 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
