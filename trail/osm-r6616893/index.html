Sproat Lake Park Trail

Sproat Lake Park Trail is a 0.5 mi trail (1.0 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 310 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 1.0 mi round trip
  • About 310 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
