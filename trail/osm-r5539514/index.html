Pass Creek Trail

Pass Creek Trail is a 3.5 mi trail (7.0 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,070 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 7.0 mi round trip
  • About 1,070 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
