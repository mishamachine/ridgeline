Bonney Meadows Trail

Bonney Meadows Trail is a 2.4 mi trail (4.8 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,610 ft of gain, computed level moderate.

  • Out and back: 4.8 mi round trip
  • About 1,610 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
