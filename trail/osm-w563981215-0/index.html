Eldorado Basin Trail

Eldorado Basin Trail is a 1.9 mi trail (3.8 mi out and back) near Newhalem, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,930 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, no dogs. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.8 mi round trip
  • About 3,930 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, no dogs
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
