Spanish Ridge Trail

Spanish Ridge Trail is a 5.1 mi trail (10 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,590 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 2,590 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
