Lake Lillian Trail

Lake Lillian Trail is a 4.0 mi trail (7.9 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,900 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.9 mi round trip
  • About 1,900 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
