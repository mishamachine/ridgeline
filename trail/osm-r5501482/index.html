Grand Ridge Trail

Grand Ridge Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.1 mi out and back) near Issaquah, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 750 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.1 mi round trip
  • About 750 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, bikes allowed, horses allowed
