Copper Basin Trail

Copper Basin Trail is a 2.8 mi trail (5.5 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,440 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 5.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,440 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
