Bald Mountain Ridge Trail

Bald Mountain Ridge Trail is a 6.3 mi trail (13 mi out and back) near Sultan, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,380 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 13 mi round trip
  • About 2,380 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
