Shaw Lake Trail

Shaw Lake Trail is a 3.4 mi trail (6.7 mi out and back) near Sultan, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,400 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.7 mi round trip
  • About 1,400 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
