Teanaway Ridge Trail

Teanaway Ridge Trail is a 4.3 mi trail (8.6 mi out and back) near Cle Elum, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,470 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.6 mi round trip
  • About 1,470 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
