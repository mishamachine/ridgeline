Bear Ridge Trail

Bear Ridge Trail is a 1.6 mi trail (3.1 mi out and back) near Issaquah, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 820 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.1 mi round trip
  • About 820 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
