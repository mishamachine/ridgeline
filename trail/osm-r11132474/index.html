Nestor Peak Hike

Nestor Peak Hike is a 3.9 mi trail (7.9 mi out and back) near Trout Lake, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,860 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.9 mi round trip
  • About 1,860 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
