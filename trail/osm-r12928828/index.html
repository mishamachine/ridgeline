Twin Lakes Trail

Twin Lakes Trail is a 4.9 mi trail (9.8 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,830 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.8 mi round trip
  • About 2,830 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
