Sulphide Glacier Route

Sulphide Glacier Route is a 2.4 mi trail (4.9 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,700 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 4.9 mi round trip
  • About 2,700 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
