Explorer Falls Trail

Explorer Falls Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.2 mi out and back) near Sultan, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 520 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.2 mi round trip
  • About 520 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
