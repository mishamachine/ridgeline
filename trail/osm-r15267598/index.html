Flapjack Lakes Trail

Flapjack Lakes Trail is a 3.6 mi trail (7.2 mi out and back) near Hoodsport, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,330 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 7.2 mi round trip
  • About 2,330 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
