Pleasant Valley Lake

Pleasant Valley Lake is a 2.3 mi trail (4.7 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,290 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 4.7 mi round trip
  • About 2,290 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
