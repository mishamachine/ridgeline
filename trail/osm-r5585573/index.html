Sulphur Mountain Trail

Sulphur Mountain Trail is a 5.2 mi trail (10 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 4,000 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 4,000 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
