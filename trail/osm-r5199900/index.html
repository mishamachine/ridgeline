Lake George Trail

Lake George Trail is a 4.5 mi trail (8.9 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,530 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.9 mi round trip
  • About 1,530 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
