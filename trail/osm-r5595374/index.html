Triad Creek Trail

Triad Creek Trail is a 3.8 mi trail (7.5 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,460 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 7.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,460 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
