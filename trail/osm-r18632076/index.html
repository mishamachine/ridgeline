Sherman Pass

Sherman Pass is a 4.1 mi trail (8.2 mi out and back) near Republic, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,400 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.2 mi round trip
  • About 1,400 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
