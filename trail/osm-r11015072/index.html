Lake Loop Trail

Lake Loop Trail is a 2.2 mi trail (4.3 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 250 ft of gain, computed level moderate.

  • Out and back: 4.3 mi round trip
  • About 250 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
