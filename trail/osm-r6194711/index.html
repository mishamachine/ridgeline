Dusty Lake Trail

Dusty Lake Trail is a 2.7 mi trail (5.4 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 170 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.4 mi round trip
  • About 170 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
