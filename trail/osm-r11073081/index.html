East Applegate Ridge Trail

East Applegate Ridge Trail is a 5.2 mi trail (10 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,370 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed, dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 1,370 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed, dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
