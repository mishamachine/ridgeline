Mount Ellinor Trail #812

Mount Ellinor Trail #812 is a 3.0 mi trail (6.0 mi out and back) near Hoodsport, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,150 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.0 mi round trip

  • Mapped highlights: a lake
