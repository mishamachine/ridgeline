LaCrosse Pass Trail

LaCrosse Pass Trail is a 5.6 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Brinnon, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,780 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 2,780 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
