Lake Ann Trail

Lake Ann Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.1 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 950 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.1 mi round trip
  • About 950 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
