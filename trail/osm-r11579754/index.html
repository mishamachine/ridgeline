Bandera Mountain Trail

Bandera Mountain Trail is a 3.3 mi trail (6.6 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,930 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 6.6 mi round trip
  • About 2,930 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: dogs allowed
