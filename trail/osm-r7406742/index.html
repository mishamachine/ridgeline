Hammill Lake Trails

Hammill Lake Trails is a 1.3 mi trail (2.6 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 210 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: a lake.

  • Out and back: 2.6 mi round trip
  • About 210 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
