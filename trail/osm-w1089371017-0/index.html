Cave Ridge Trail

Cave Ridge Trail is a 0.6 mi trail (1.3 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 620 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 1.3 mi round trip
  • About 620 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
