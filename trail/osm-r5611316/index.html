Clearwater Peak Trail

Clearwater Peak Trail is a 1.1 mi trail (2.2 mi out and back) near Enumclaw, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 880 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.2 mi round trip
  • About 880 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead

Open in Ridgeline

Directions are a guide; check conditions; not for emergency use.
