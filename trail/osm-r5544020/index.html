Pass Lake Trail

Pass Lake Trail is a 4.9 mi trail (9.8 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,400 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a waterfall. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.8 mi round trip
  • About 2,400 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a waterfall
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
