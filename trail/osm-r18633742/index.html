Midnight Ridge

Midnight Ridge is a 4.0 mi trail (8.1 mi out and back) near Republic, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,150 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.1 mi round trip
  • About 2,150 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
