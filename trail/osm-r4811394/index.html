Pete Lake Trail

Pete Lake Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.3 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 370 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.3 mi round trip
  • About 370 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
