Comet Falls Trail

Comet Falls Trail is a 1.8 mi trail (3.5 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,540 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,540 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
