Hinckley Basin Fire Road

Hinckley Basin Fire Road is a 3.1 mi trail (6.2 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,300 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed.

  • Out and back: 6.2 mi round trip
  • About 1,300 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed
