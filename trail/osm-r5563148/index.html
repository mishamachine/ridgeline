Kendall Peak Lakes Trail

Kendall Peak Lakes Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.3 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,890 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.3 mi round trip
  • About 1,890 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
