Tolmie Peak Trail

Tolmie Peak Trail is a 3.1 mi trail (6.3 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,220 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.3 mi round trip
  • About 1,220 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
