Four Mile Ridge Trail

Four Mile Ridge Trail is a 5.1 mi trail (10 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,330 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 2,330 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
