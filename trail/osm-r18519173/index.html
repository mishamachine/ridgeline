Lake to Lake Trail

Lake to Lake Trail is a 6.1 mi trail (12 mi out and back) near Vancouver, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 190 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall.

  • Out and back: 12 mi round trip
  • About 190 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall
