Lake Twenty Two Trail (Lake 22)

Lake Twenty Two Trail (Lake 22) is a 3.6 mi trail (7.2 mi out and back) near Granite Falls, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,340 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.2 mi round trip
  • About 1,340 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
