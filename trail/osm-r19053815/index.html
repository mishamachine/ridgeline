Iron Lake Trail

Iron Lake Trail is a 3.6 mi trail (7.2 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,290 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 7.2 mi round trip
  • About 2,290 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
