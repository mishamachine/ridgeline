Grand Ridge via Lake Eleanor

Grand Ridge via Lake Eleanor is a 4.2 mi trail (8.3 mi out and back) near Enumclaw, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,270 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.3 mi round trip
  • About 1,270 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
