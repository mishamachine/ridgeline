Thompson Point Trail

Thompson Point Trail is a 2.7 mi trail (5.4 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,020 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall.

  • Out and back: 5.4 mi round trip
  • About 2,020 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall
