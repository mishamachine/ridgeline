Wynoochee Pass Trail

Wynoochee Pass Trail is a 3.3 mi trail (6.7 mi out and back) near Lake Quinault, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 980 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 6.7 mi round trip
  • About 980 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
