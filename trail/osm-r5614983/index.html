East Ridge Trail

East Ridge Trail is a 1.7 mi trail (3.5 mi out and back) near Issaquah, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,050 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall.

  • Out and back: 3.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,050 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall
