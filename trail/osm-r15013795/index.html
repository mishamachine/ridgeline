Pyramid Mountain Trail

Pyramid Mountain Trail is a 3.3 mi trail (6.6 mi out and back) near Port Angeles, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,330 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.6 mi round trip
  • About 2,330 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
