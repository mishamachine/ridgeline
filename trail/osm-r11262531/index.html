Kangaroo Lake Fen Trail

Kangaroo Lake Fen Trail is a 1.2 mi trail (2.4 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 620 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.4 mi round trip
  • About 620 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
