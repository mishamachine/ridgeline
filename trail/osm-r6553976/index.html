Basin Lake Trail

Basin Lake Trail is a 2.8 mi trail (5.5 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 950 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 5.5 mi round trip
  • About 950 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
