Owyhigh Lakes Trail

Owyhigh Lakes Trail is a 3.6 mi trail (7.1 mi out and back) near Packwood, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,550 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: no dogs.

  • Out and back: 7.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,550 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: no dogs
