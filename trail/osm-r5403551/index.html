Badger Lake Trail

Badger Lake Trail is a 0.7 mi trail (1.4 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 250 ft of gain, computed level easy. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 1.4 mi round trip
  • About 250 ft gain (one way up), level easy (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
