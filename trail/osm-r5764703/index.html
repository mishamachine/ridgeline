Lost Lake Trail

Lost Lake Trail is a 4.2 mi trail (8.3 mi out and back) near Leavenworth, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 920 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.3 mi round trip
  • About 920 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
