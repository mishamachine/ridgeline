Meander Meadow - Dishpan Gap - Cady Ridge Loop

Meander Meadow - Dishpan Gap - Cady Ridge Loop is a 2.4 mi trail (4.7 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 410 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.
