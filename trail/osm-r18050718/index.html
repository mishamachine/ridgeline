Bridal Veil Falls Trail

Bridal Veil Falls Trail is a 2.0 mi trail (3.9 mi out and back) near Index, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,040 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.9 mi round trip
  • About 1,040 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
