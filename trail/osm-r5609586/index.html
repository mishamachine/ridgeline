Deep Creek Trail to Mutton Mountain

Deep Creek Trail to Mutton Mountain is a 4.3 mi trail (8.7 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,340 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.7 mi round trip
  • About 3,340 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
