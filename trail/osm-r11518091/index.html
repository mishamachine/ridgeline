Crystal Mountain Trail

Crystal Mountain Trail is a 3.2 mi trail (6.4 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,250 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 6.4 mi round trip
  • About 1,250 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
