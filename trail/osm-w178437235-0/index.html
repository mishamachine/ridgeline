Lucky ridge boot trail

Lucky ridge boot trail is a 2.2 mi trail (4.4 mi out and back) near Newhalem, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,970 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 4.4 mi round trip
  • About 3,970 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
