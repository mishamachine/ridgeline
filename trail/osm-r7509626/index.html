Duffy Lake Trail

Duffy Lake Trail is a 2.3 mi trail (4.5 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 540 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.5 mi round trip
  • About 540 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
