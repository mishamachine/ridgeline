Rainbow Lake Trail

Rainbow Lake Trail is a 1.5 mi trail (3.1 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 510 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 3.1 mi round trip
  • About 510 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
