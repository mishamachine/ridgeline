Old Sauk Trail

Old Sauk Trail is a 3.0 mi trail (6.1 mi out and back) near Darrington, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 420 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.1 mi round trip
  • About 420 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
