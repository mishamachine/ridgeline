Blackjack Ridge Trail

Blackjack Ridge Trail is a 7.4 mi trail (15 mi out and back) near Leavenworth, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 4,360 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, a beach, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 15 mi round trip
  • About 4,360 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, a beach, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
