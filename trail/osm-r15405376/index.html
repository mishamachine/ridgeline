Vineyard Mountain Trail

Vineyard Mountain Trail is a 1.4 mi trail (2.9 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 630 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.9 mi round trip
  • About 630 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
