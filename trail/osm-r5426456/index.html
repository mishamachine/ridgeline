Lake Clarice Trail

Lake Clarice Trail is a 4.2 mi trail (8.3 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 900 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 8.3 mi round trip
  • About 900 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
