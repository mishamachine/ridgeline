Easton Ridge Trail

Easton Ridge Trail is a 4.0 mi trail (8.0 mi out and back) near Cle Elum, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,050 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.0 mi round trip
  • About 2,050 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
