Meysan Lakes Trail

Meysan Lakes Trail is a 4.6 mi trail (9.2 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,690 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 9.2 mi round trip
  • About 3,690 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
