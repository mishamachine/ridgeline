Lynn Peak Route

Lynn Peak Route is a 1.9 mi trail (3.8 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,050 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall, dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 3.8 mi round trip
  • About 2,050 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall, dogs allowed
