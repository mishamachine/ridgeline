Sunol Ridge Trail

Sunol Ridge Trail is a 4.2 mi trail (8.5 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,890 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.5 mi round trip
  • About 1,890 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
