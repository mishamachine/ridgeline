Granite Mountain Trail

Granite Mountain Trail is a 4.0 mi trail (7.9 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,690 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.9 mi round trip
  • About 3,690 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
