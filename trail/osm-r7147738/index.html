Mailbox Peak Old Trail

Mailbox Peak Old Trail is a 2.4 mi trail (4.9 mi out and back) near North Bend, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,930 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.9 mi round trip
  • About 3,930 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
