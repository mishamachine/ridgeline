Bugaboo Ridge Trail

Bugaboo Ridge Trail is a 3.0 mi trail (6.0 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,270 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.0 mi round trip
  • About 1,270 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
