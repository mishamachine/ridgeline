Poe Mountain Trail

Poe Mountain Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.2 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,890 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.2 mi round trip
  • About 2,890 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
