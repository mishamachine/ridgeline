Davis Peak Trail

Davis Peak Trail is a 4.5 mi trail (9.0 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,890 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.0 mi round trip
  • About 3,890 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
