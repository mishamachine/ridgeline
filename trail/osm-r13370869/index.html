Saltese Ridge Trail

Saltese Ridge Trail is a 1.7 mi trail (3.4 mi out and back) near Spokane, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 680 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed, dogs allowed.

  • Out and back: 3.4 mi round trip
  • About 680 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed, dogs allowed
