Minotaur Lake Trail

Minotaur Lake Trail is a 1.5 mi trail (2.9 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,830 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 2.9 mi round trip
  • About 1,830 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
