Maple Pass Loop Trail

Maple Pass Loop Trail is a 6.7 mi loop near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,000 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Loop: 6.7 mi around
  • About 2,000 ft gain, level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
