Sawtooth Mountain Trail #107

Sawtooth Mountain Trail #107 is a 1.6 mi trail (3.3 mi out and back) near Trout Lake, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 560 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.
