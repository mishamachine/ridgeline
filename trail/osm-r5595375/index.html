Gamma Ridge Trail

Gamma Ridge Trail is a 4.1 mi trail (8.1 mi out and back) near Stehekin, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 3,020 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 8.1 mi round trip
  • About 3,020 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
