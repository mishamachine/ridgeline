Bailey Range Way Trail

Bailey Range Way Trail is a 2.7 mi trail (5.4 mi out and back) near Port Angeles, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,200 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 5.4 mi round trip
