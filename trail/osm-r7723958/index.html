Mount Whitney Mountaineer's Route

Mount Whitney Mountaineer's Route is a 4.7 mi trail (9.5 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 6,030 ft of gain, computed level hard. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 9.5 mi round trip
  • About 6,030 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
