Denny Creek Trail

Denny Creek Trail is a 4.4 mi trail (8.8 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,320 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 8.8 mi round trip
  • About 2,320 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, dogs allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
