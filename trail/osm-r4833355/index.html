Park Butte Trail

Park Butte Trail is a 3.7 mi trail (7.5 mi out and back) near Concrete, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,030 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,030 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, a waterfall, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
