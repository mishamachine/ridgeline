Deer Creek Summit Trail

Deer Creek Summit Trail is a 1.6 mi trail (3.2 mi out and back) near Republic, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 660 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 3.2 mi round trip
  • About 660 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
