Section Line Trail

Section Line Trail is a 1.6 mi trail (3.2 mi out and back) near Issaquah, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,010 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake.

  • Out and back: 3.2 mi round trip
  • About 2,010 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
