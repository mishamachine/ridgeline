U.S. Mountain Trail

U.S. Mountain Trail is a 2.8 mi trail (5.5 mi out and back) near Republic, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 900 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake, bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 5.5 mi round trip
  • About 900 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake, bikes allowed, horses allowed
