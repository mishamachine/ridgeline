Humpback Mountain Trail

Humpback Mountain Trail is a 2.0 mi trail (4.0 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,570 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.0 mi round trip
  • About 2,570 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
