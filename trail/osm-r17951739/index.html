Schaefer Lake Trail

Schaefer Lake Trail is a 4.3 mi trail (8.6 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,700 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 8.6 mi round trip
  • About 2,700 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
