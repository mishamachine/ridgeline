Shoestring Lake Trail

Shoestring Lake Trail is a 3.8 mi trail (7.6 mi out and back) near Cle Elum, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,070 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 7.6 mi round trip
  • About 1,070 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
