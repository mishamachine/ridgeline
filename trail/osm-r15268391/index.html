Pine Lake Trail

Pine Lake Trail is a 2.7 mi trail (5.4 mi out and back) near Hoodsport, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 900 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 5.4 mi round trip
  • About 900 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
