Worm Flows Climbing Route

Worm Flows Climbing Route is a 5.2 mi trail (10 mi out and back) near Cougar, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 5,580 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 5,580 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a waterfall
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
