Antoine Summit Trail

Antoine Summit Trail is a 3.3 mi trail (6.6 mi out and back) near Spokane, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 770 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 6.6 mi round trip
  • About 770 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
