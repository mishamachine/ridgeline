Whittaker Wilderness Peak Trail

Whittaker Wilderness Peak Trail is a 2.1 mi trail (4.1 mi out and back) near Issaquah, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,270 ft of gain, computed level moderate. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.1 mi round trip
  • About 1,270 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
