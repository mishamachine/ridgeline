Striped Peak Trail

Striped Peak Trail is a 2.4 mi trail (4.7 mi out and back) near Port Angeles, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,010 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 4.7 mi round trip
  • About 1,010 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
