Minnow Ridge ORV Trail

Minnow Ridge ORV Trail is a 6.0 mi trail (12 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,110 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 12 mi round trip
  • About 1,110 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
