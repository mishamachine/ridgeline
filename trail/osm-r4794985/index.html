Dingford Creek Trail

Dingford Creek Trail is a 5.0 mi trail (10 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,290 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, a waterfall, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 10 mi round trip
  • About 2,290 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, a waterfall, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
