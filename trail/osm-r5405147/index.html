Forest Creek Trail

Forest Creek Trail is a 3.1 mi trail (6.2 mi out and back) in Oregon, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 960 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed.

  • Out and back: 6.2 mi round trip
  • About 960 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
