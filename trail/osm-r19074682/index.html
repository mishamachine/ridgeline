Harrison Pass Trail

Harrison Pass Trail is a 4.7 mi trail (9.5 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,870 ft of gain, computed level hard.

  • Out and back: 9.5 mi round trip
  • About 2,870 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
