Eagle Lake Fisherman's Trail

Eagle Lake Fisherman's Trail is a 1.9 mi trail (3.7 mi out and back) near Skykomish, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 610 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: views, a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.
