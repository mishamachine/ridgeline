Skyline Divide Trail

Skyline Divide Trail is a 5.3 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Glacier, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,450 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 2,450 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
