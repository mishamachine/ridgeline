Melakwa Lake Trail

Melakwa Lake Trail is a 2.6 mi trail (5.1 mi out and back) near Snoqualmie Pass, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,170 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake, dogs allowed.
