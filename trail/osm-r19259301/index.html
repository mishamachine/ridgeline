Woss Lookout Trail

Woss Lookout Trail is a 0.6 mi trail (1.2 mi out and back) in British Columbia, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 830 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 1.2 mi round trip
  • About 830 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
