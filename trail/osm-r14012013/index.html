Ridge Trail

Ridge Trail is a 1.8 mi trail (3.7 mi out and back) in California, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 790 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: horses allowed, no dogs.

  • Out and back: 3.7 mi round trip
  • About 790 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: horses allowed, no dogs
