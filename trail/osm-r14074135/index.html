Pyramid Peak via Longmire

Pyramid Peak via Longmire is a 7.0 mi trail (14 mi out and back) near Ashford, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 2,880 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: a lake. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 14 mi round trip
  • About 2,880 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: a lake
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
