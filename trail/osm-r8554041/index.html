North Lost Lake Trail

North Lost Lake Trail is a 5.4 mi trail (11 mi out and back) near Bellingham, WA, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 1,410 ft of gain, computed level hard. Mapped highlights: views, a lake, bikes allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 11 mi round trip
  • About 1,410 ft gain (one way up), level hard (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: views, a lake, bikes allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
