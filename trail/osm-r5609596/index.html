Dalles Ridge Trail

Dalles Ridge Trail is a 1.8 mi trail (3.5 mi out and back) in Washington, mapped in OpenStreetMap. About 500 ft of gain, computed level moderate. Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed. A car park is mapped at the trailhead.

  • Out and back: 3.5 mi round trip
  • About 500 ft gain (one way up), level moderate (computed from distance and climb)
  • Mapped highlights: bikes allowed, horses allowed
  • Car park mapped at the trailhead
