Ridgeline Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Last updated 8 November 2026

The short version. Ridgeline has no ads, and we don't sell your data. You can use it without an account, and then your location, recorded hikes and saved trails stay on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we store your email address, the name and picture Google or Apple share with us, your saved trails, hike history and the routes you walked (minus the first and last 200 m) so they're on all your devices. See Accounts below. To show maps, trails and places, your device asks a few outside services for data, and they see your IP address and what you asked for.

Who we are. Ridgeline is made by B&B Project Factory, run by Babar Ali in Washington State, USA. (Bobo is our mascot.) Contact: our support page.

What stays on your device

  • Your location. Ridgeline uses your location to centre the map when you tap Near me or Locate, and your precise location while you navigate or record. In the web version, location stops when your screen locks, so Ridgeline keeps the screen on while you navigate or record. It stops when you finish. In the Ridgeline apps, directions keep working with the screen off during a hike you start; Android shows a notification and iPhone shows the blue location indicator while this runs. Your live location is never sent to our servers. If you're signed in, the route of a finished hike is saved to your account (minus the first and last 200 m), unless you turn on 'Keep hike tracks on this phone only'.
  • Recorded hikes and GPX files. These are saved on your device. If you're not signed in, they leave it only if you choose to export them. If you're signed in, your hike history and routes (minus the first and last 200 m) also sync to our servers (see Accounts).
  • Saved trails and offline maps. Offline maps are stored on your device only. If you're signed in, your list of saved trails also syncs to your account.

If you're not signed in, clearing the site's data in your browser (or deleting the app) deletes all of this, and we can't recover it, because we never had it. If you're signed in, your account copy stays until you delete it (see Deleting).

Services your device contacts

To work, Ridgeline fetches data from these services. Each sees your IP address and the request, under its own privacy policy:

  • OpenFreeMap, for map tiles (it can tell which area you are viewing).
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap), for trail data in areas we don't host ourselves.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap), for place search (it sees the text you search for).
  • Apple Maps or Google Maps, only when you tap Drive to parking or Drive to trail start. We pass them the trailhead's location (the parking lot or trail start, not where you are). From there they work under their own privacy policy.
  • Supabase (our database, United States/Oregon), only when you sign in.
  • Google or Apple sign-in, only when you sign in.
  • Our web hosts (Hostinger and GitHub Pages), which keep standard server logs: IP address, browser type and pages loaded.

Usage counts

We don't run a visit counter today. If we add one, it will use no cookies, store nothing on your device and record no IDs or location, and we'll name it here. We also use Google Search Console, which reports how our site appears in Google search and contains no data from the app.

Crash reports

None.

Spoken directions

Spoken directions use your device's text-to-speech. Ridgeline asks for a voice installed on your device. If your browser has none, it uses the browser's default voice, which some browsers may run online, sending the words to be spoken to the browser maker's service.

Exporting a hike

You can export a recorded hike as a GPX file. It is made on your device and goes only where you choose to send it.

Sharing a hike

At the end of a hike you can make a share card. The card is made on your device and goes out through your phone's share sheet, to wherever you choose. By default it doesn't show your route or where you started. If you choose to show your route, the first and last 200 m are hidden unless you turn that off. No coordinates are on the card. Links on the card and in your message point to public trail pages (hike.bbprojectfactory.com/h/...) and carry only the trail and your hike's distance, climb, time and whether you finished: not your name or your GPS position. The card shows the trail name, the date and your stats.

What we don't do

Accounts are optional. No payments, no ads, no marketing email, no sale or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across other apps or sites.

Children

Ridgeline is for a general audience. It's not meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If we learn that a child under that age has an account, we delete it.

Accounts (optional)

Accounts (optional). You can use Ridgeline without an account. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we store:

  • your email address, and the name and profile picture Google or Apple share with us
  • the trails you save and shortlist
  • your hike history (date, time, distance, climb, the trail, and the route you walked, with the first and last 200 m removed)
  • your settings

We use these only to keep your hikes safe and on all your devices. You can keep routes on your phone only (Profile › Settings › “Keep hike tracks on this phone only”).

One account across our apps. Your sign-in is one account shared with our other apps (B&B and Bobo Learns). Signing in to Ridgeline doesn't share your hikes with those apps. If you sign in with Google and Apple using the same email address, they become one account (this doesn't happen if you chose Apple's “Hide My Email”). Signing in doesn't add you to any mailing list or create anything in our other apps.

Who stores it. Supabase (our database provider) stores your data in the United States (Oregon). It's encrypted in transit. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for ads.

Deleting. In Profile › Account or at hike.bbprojectfactory.com/account/delete/ you can delete your Ridgeline data only, or your whole account across our apps. Deleting removes your data from our live systems, normally straight away and always within 7 days; backup copies are erased within 7 days, when backups roll over. If you signed in with Apple on an iPhone, we also tell Apple to disconnect Ridgeline. If you signed in with Apple on the web, or that doesn't work, to fully disconnect, also remove Ridgeline in your Apple ID settings › Sign in with Apple (appleid.apple.com).

Usage counts are separate. Usage counts are never linked to your account.

Your choices

You can turn off location in your device settings (navigation and recording then won't work), choose not to export hikes, and delete everything: if you're not signed in, by removing the app or clearing site data; if you're signed in, in Profile › Account or at hike.bbprojectfactory.com/account/delete/.

Safety

Directions and trail data are a guide only. Conditions change: check them before you go. Ridgeline is not for emergency use.
