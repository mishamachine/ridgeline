Ridgeline Support

Ridgeline is a free hiking app with offline-first trail maps for Washington. No account, no sign-in. Save a trail to your phone and it works with no signal.

Directions are a guide; check conditions; not for emergency use.

Common questions

How do I save a trail for offline use?

Open a trail and tap the save-offline button before you leave home, on Wi-Fi. Ridgeline downloads the map for that trail to your device. Saved trails are listed on the offline screen, where you can remove them to free space.

The GPS dot is slow or jumps around. Why?

GPS needs a clear view of the sky and can take a minute to settle after you open the app, especially in forest, canyons or bad weather. Ridgeline shows the current accuracy (for example "GPS ±30 ft") while you navigate, and ignores fixes that are too rough when recording. Wait for the accuracy figure to drop before you rely on the dot, and keep location turned on in your device settings.

Will directions keep working with the screen off?

No. In the web version, the browser stops location when the screen locks, so Ridgeline asks your device to keep the screen on while you navigate (where your browser supports it). Keep the page open and the screen on.

How do I delete my data?

Ridgeline keeps no account and nothing on our servers. Your recorded hikes and saved trails live on your device only. Delete a single hike or saved trail from its own screen, or delete everything by removing the app or clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

Why does Ridgeline ask for location?

To centre the map on you, guide you along a trail and record your hike. It uses your location to centre the map when you open Ridgeline or tap Locate, and your precise location while you navigate or record a hike you started. Your location is never sent to us. You can turn it off in your device settings; navigation and recording then won't work. See the privacy policy for the full list.
